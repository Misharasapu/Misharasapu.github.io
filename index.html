About

I studied mechanical engineering at the University of Bristol, then an MSc in Data Science there, which I finished with Distinction, and I now build machine learning systems, most recently a computer vision prototype at Brompton Bicycle.

My engineering degree ended with a reinforcement learning project on gas networks, and my MSc dissertation with SYNOPTIX tested whether synthetic images could stand in for real ones in fire detection. I focus on building machine learning solutions that are explainable, grounded in real operational constraints, and useful to the people who rely on them.
